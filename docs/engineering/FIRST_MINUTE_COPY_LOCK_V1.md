## 3. Locked Strings — Fast Reflection Screen (FM-06)

### 3.1 Screen Header

**LOCKED:**
> What I'm seeing so far

**Rationale:** "I'm seeing" is human-register and implies a person noticing, not a system classifying. "So far" communicates incompleteness as a feature, not a limitation.

**Prohibited alternatives:**
- "Analysis results" — system-register, fails P5
- "Here's what we found" — "we found" is extractive, not observational
- "Your essay assessment" — positions system as a judge
- "AI observations" — violates P6

---

### 3.2 Primary CTA

**LOCKED:**
> Show me the strongest direction

---

### 3.3 Secondary CTA (shown when next_question is non-null)

**LOCKED:**
> Ask me one clarifying question first

---

### 3.4 Observation Register Rules

Observations must:
- Start with "I'm seeing…", "There may be…", "This looks like…", "What keeps coming through is…", "The interesting part is…"
- Be one sentence
- Be specific to the content (never generic)
- Name the narrative pattern in plain language, never by taxonomy label

Observations must never:
- Start with "We detected…", "Analysis shows…", "The AI…", "Based on your input…"
- Include any score, percentage, or confidence value
- Use taxonomy labels ("self_correction_arc", "identity_shift", etc.)
- Exceed one sentence per observation

## 10. Voice Reference

### Correct register (every output should sound like this):
> The stronger story is not the activity itself. It is the moment your understanding changed.

> Most essays about debate focus on wins. This one could be about what changed in the room when you didn't.

> There's a correction moment here. The story gets more interesting after the feedback, not before.

### Incorrect register (never ship anything that sounds like this):
> This compelling narrative beautifully showcases your authentic growth and resilience.

> Our AI has detected a strong personal growth arc in your unique story.

> Great work! Your essay demonstrates impressive leadership skills and maturity.
