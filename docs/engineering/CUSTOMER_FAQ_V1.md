# CUSTOMER_FAQ_V1

## The College Admissions Edge

## Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. What is The College Admissions Edge?

The College Admissions Edge is a guided admissions-writing and planning platform designed to help students build stronger college application materials with more structure, clarity, and confidence.

It helps students:

* discover stronger personal story themes
* develop a more authentic personal statement
* organize school-specific supplements
* track school lists and deadlines
* give families progress visibility without turning the process into chaos

The goal is not to replace the student’s voice. The goal is to help students uncover it and communicate it more clearly.

---

## 2. Who is this for?

The College Admissions Edge is built for:

* students applying to college
* families who want more structure and visibility
* students who want real guidance without paying for a full luxury admissions consulting experience

It is especially useful for students who have strong potential but need help turning their experiences into compelling application writing.

---

## 3. Does the app write my essay for me?

No.

The College Admissions Edge is not a ghostwriting tool.

The platform helps students:

* identify strong themes
* choose better directions
* build outlines
* improve drafts
* get structured feedback

But the student is still expected to be the author.

That matters for authenticity, for trust, and for the quality of the final application.

---

## 4. How is this different from just using a generic AI chatbot?

Generic AI tools can produce broad writing help, but they are usually not structured around the college admissions process.

The College Admissions Edge is designed specifically for that process. It is organized around:

* student story discovery
* personal statement development
* supplement planning
* school and deadline context
* family visibility where appropriate

It is built as a workflow, not just a chat box.

---

## 5. How does the free Edge Snapshot work?

The free Edge Snapshot is the starting point for the student experience.

After onboarding, the app uses the student’s responses to generate a structured summary that highlights:

* likely strongest themes
* possible story directions
* potential gaps or missing elements
* a clearer sense of what may make the student stand out

It is designed to give students and families an early sense of where the strongest application story may be.

---

## 6. Is the Edge Snapshot instant?

Usually it should complete quickly, but it is not guaranteed to be instant every time.

Some parts of the experience involve structured generation and processing, so users may occasionally see an in-progress or loading state before a result is ready.

---

## 7. What do I get with the paid version?

Paid access unlocks the core writing and planning workflows, including:

* Story Vault
* personal statement workspace
* supplement workspace
* fuller school planning experience
* supporting-adult dashboard access where applicable
* premium progress and guidance features

The paid version is designed for students who want to move beyond discovery into real application-building work.

---

## 8. What is Story Vault?

Story Vault is the student’s private workspace for collecting experiences, themes, accomplishments, challenges, and moments that may become useful across the application.

It helps students move from:

* random memories
* scattered thoughts
* unfinished ideas

to a clearer bank of stories and angles they can reuse intelligently.

---

## 9. What is the personal statement workspace?

The personal statement workspace helps students move through a structured essay process:

* choose a narrative direction
* generate outline options
* draft the essay
* receive structured feedback
* revise over time

The focus is on helping students build a more authentic and coherent essay, not on auto-generating a final answer to submit without thought.

---

## 10. What is the supplements workspace?

The supplements workspace helps students organize and write school-specific responses.

It is designed to:

* group prompts by school
* help students choose better angles for each prompt
* reduce repetitive answers across applications
* connect supplement work back to school planning

---

## 11. Does the app help prevent repeating the same story too many times?

Yes.

One of the goals of the supplement workflow is to help students avoid saying the same thing over and over across the personal statement and multiple school-specific responses.

The platform can surface overlap risk and help students differentiate their angles more clearly.

---

## 12. Can parents or guardians see the student’s writing?

Not as raw draft access in v1.

The supporting-adult experience is designed around summary visibility, not direct editorial control.

Supporting adults may be able to see:

* progress summaries
* milestone status
* deadline summaries

They do not get raw access to student Story Vault entries or direct in-app editing of student writing in v1.

---

## 13. Can parents edit the student’s essay inside the app?

No.

In v1, writing workflows remain student-owned inside the app.

That is intentional. The platform is designed to support student authorship rather than turn the process into a shared editing environment.

---

## 14. How does the parent or supporting-adult view work?

The parent/supporting-adult experience is based on an explicit account link.

Once linked, the supporting adult may be able to view:

* progress summaries
* key milestones
* school/deadline summaries

This is meant to provide visibility and support without exposing the student’s full private writing workspace.

---

## 15. Do I need to link accounts for a parent or guardian to see progress?

Yes.

Supporting-adult visibility depends on a completed account link. The system does not assume family relationships automatically.

---

## 16. Does the app sync directly with Common App?

No.

v1 does not directly sync with a user’s Common App account or other live admissions portal accounts.

The school planner is an internal planning tool. It helps students organize schools, deadlines, and application work inside the app, but it is not a direct portal integration.

---

## 17. Can I submit my applications through this app?

No.

The College Admissions Edge helps students prepare stronger materials and stay organized, but it is not an application submission platform.

Students still submit through the appropriate external admissions systems.

---

## 18. Does the app include a list of colleges and deadlines?

Yes, the app is designed to support structured school and deadline data inside the planner.

That said, some records may be:

* fully verified
* partially complete
* in need of direct source confirmation

The goal is to provide strong practical planning support while being honest about data confidence.

---

## 19. Are all deadlines guaranteed to be perfect and live-updated?

No.

The app is built to provide strong school and deadline support, but not every record should be assumed to be perfect in real time for every institution.

When necessary, students should verify critical deadlines directly with the institution source.

---

## 20. What if I see a deadline that looks wrong?

If a specific school or deadline looks incorrect, that should be reviewed directly for that institution and admissions cycle.

The best next step is to flag the specific school and date rather than assume the entire planner is wrong.

---

## 21. Does the app work on mobile?

Yes, v1 is designed as a responsive web app.

That means it should work across desktop and mobile browsers, but it is not a native iOS or Android app in v1.

---

## 22. Is there a native mobile app?

Not in v1.

The current version is a web application built to work responsively across devices.

---

## 23. What happens if I pay and the premium features still look locked?

If payment completed but premium features still appear locked, that usually points to a billing-state or entitlement-sync issue.

That should be reviewed directly rather than guessed at.

The correct next step is to verify the subscription and access state tied to the account.

---

## 24. Why can one linked person see billing controls and another cannot?

Billing visibility depends on account ownership.

In v1, billing controls are tied to the billing owner account. A linked non-owner may not see the billing portal or the same billing controls.

---

## 25. Does the app guarantee admission results?

No.

No responsible admissions product can guarantee an admissions result.

The College Admissions Edge is designed to improve structure, clarity, and application quality. It can help students present themselves more strongly, but it does not guarantee acceptance outcomes.

---

## 26. Can the app tell me the perfect essay topic?

Not in an absolute sense.

The platform can help identify strong directions, weak spots, and more promising story paths. But it is still a guidance system, not an all-knowing admissions oracle.

The strength of the result also depends on the quality and depth of the student’s input.

---

## 27. What if the AI feedback feels too broad or not strong enough?

AI guidance depends partly on the quality and detail of the student’s responses and draft.

If the underlying input is vague, shallow, or incomplete, the resulting feedback may also be less specific.

The platform is designed to give structured guidance, but stronger student input usually produces stronger output.

---

## 28. What if the app feels like it is still loading or generating?

Some workflows involve structured generation, analysis, or retrieval, so certain steps may show an in-progress state before a result is ready.

If a workflow remains stuck or repeatedly fails, that should be treated as a product issue rather than normal waiting.

---

## 29. Will I get reminder emails?

You may receive reminder or progress-related emails depending on:

* your activity state
* your notification eligibility
* your account and role settings
* your notification preferences where applicable

These reminders are meant to help users stay on track, but they are not a substitute for the user’s own responsibility to review deadlines and progress.

---

## 30. What if I expected an email reminder and did not get one?

A missing reminder may be caused by:

* preference settings
* reminder eligibility timing
* delivery issues
* a workflow that had not yet triggered the reminder condition

That should be checked directly rather than assumed.

---

## 31. What if I get duplicate reminders?

Duplicate reminders should be treated seriously and reviewed directly.

If that happens, it likely points to a reminder or deduplication issue rather than normal product behavior.

---

## 32. Is this a replacement for a full private admissions counselor?

Not in the full-service, one-on-one sense.

The College Admissions Edge is built to give families a more affordable, structured edge without requiring a full luxury counseling engagement.

It offers guidance, workflow, writing support, and visibility — but it is still a product, not a full done-for-you counseling service.

---

## 33. Is this only for top students?

No.

The platform is built for families and students who want stronger structure, stronger writing, and a better chance to present themselves well.

The idea is not that only elite students deserve support. The idea is that more students deserve access to stronger support.

---

## 34. What should I do if something looks wrong in my account?

The best first step is to identify where the issue happens:

* before login
* after login
* inside a specific module
* in billing or access
* in the school/deadline planner

The more specific the issue is, the easier it is to verify and resolve accurately.

---

## 35. How should I think about v1 overall?

v1 is designed to be a strong, modern, structured first version of the platform.

It is intentionally focused on the highest-value workflows:

* story discovery
* personal statement support
* supplements support
* planning and deadline visibility
* family summary visibility

It does not try to do everything at once. It is built to do the most important things well and to expand responsibly over time.

---

## 36. Still have a question?

If something is unclear, the best next step is to contact support with the exact issue, page, or workflow involved.

Clear details help separate:

* a normal product boundary
* a settings or account issue
* a real bug that needs review

---

## 37. Final note

The College Admissions Edge is built around a simple belief:

Students need structure, confidence, and stronger guidance — not more noise.

The platform is designed to help students tell a more authentic, more compelling story while keeping families better informed and better organized along the way.
